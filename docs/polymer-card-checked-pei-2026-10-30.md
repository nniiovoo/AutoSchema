## Why the sample links are supported

The matrix film is prepared by casting the PTMG-HDI-BHDS solution and evaporating solvent (Results paragraph 9). The strength sentence names PTMG-HDI-BHDS and reports **88.3 MPa** and **2000%** (Results paragraph 10). The separate electrolyte preparation explicitly mixes the polymer with LiFSI before casting and drying (Methods paragraph 27). The conductivity sentence explicitly names **PTMG-HDI-BHDS/LiFSI**, reporting **2.4 × 10⁻⁴ S cm⁻¹ at 30°C** (Results paragraph 13). These preparation and measurement passages are both attached. “Matrix film” here means no LiFSI addition is reported for that preparation, not measured zero salt content or chemical purity. [Primary paper, Fig.2d–e, Fig.3a–b and preparation Methods](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-43467-w).

The monitoring record keeps **Mn 7.3 × 10⁵ g mol⁻¹** on BHDS, not the PTMG-HDI comparator. The source attributes this to an **8 h polymerization reaction at 40°C**; GPC uses DMF according to Methods paragraph 29. Those are reaction conditions and GPC eluent, respectively. The source does not establish a GPC instrument temperature; the card does not label 40°C as that temperature. The monitoring solution is separate from the tensile film. The 8 h monitoring observation is also kept distinct from the 12 h stirring step reported in the synthesis method; no identical-batch or identical-protocol assumption is made. [Primary paper, Fig.2b and material-characterization Methods](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-43467-w).

The voltage statement remains **“beyond 5.0 V”**, not exact 5.0 V. The attached LSV method is 10 mV s⁻¹, 0–6 V, room temperature. This is a voltage observation under the reported LSV conditions, not a certified cell operating voltage; the main-text method does not establish the asymmetric-cell electrode materials, so none are invented. The dimensionless **Li⁺ transference number 0.81** is attached to the salt electrolyte with the stated symmetric Li||Li / 50 mV polarization method. [Primary paper, Fig.3e–f and electrochemical Methods](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-43467-w).

## Focused evaluation cases

The [six evaluation cases](../examples/polymer_cards/checked_pei_2026-10-30/evaluation-cases.json) are derived directly from the checked record. They compare original values/units, the parent polymer label, the selected specimen form, required conditions and associated source locations. They do not rely on exact property-name wording or compare unrelated evidence from other facts. Value/unit comparison uses Unicode/whitespace normalization only: a different but scientifically equivalent unit/value layout can therefore be an unmatched reference notation, not a proven extraction error. These cases are never supplied to model prompts. The separate six-group key list documents this one card’s contract; it does not require inventing information for every material.

## Limits and validation

The reference populates all six groups but does not manufacture missing information. Its [explicit unknowns](../examples/polymer_cards/checked_pei_2026-10-30/reference.json) include tensile-test temperature, specimen dimensions and replicate/error information, GPC instrument temperature/flow rate, isolated yield and residual-solvent amount. The paper calls 5 cm min⁻¹ a “strain rate”; the card preserves that reported wording instead of silently converting it into normalized strain per second. The reported monomer-feed ratio is not presented as a separately measured final incorporated repeat-unit composition. No electrochemical 30°C default is transferred to mechanical testing. Qualitative film self-healing is not turned into a numerical healing efficiency. No supplementary material, graph digitization or independent experimental reproduction was used. [Primary Methods](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-43467-w).
