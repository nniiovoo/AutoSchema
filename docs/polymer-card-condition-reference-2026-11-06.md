# Source checks before the fresh extraction

The [evaluation-only reference](../examples/polymer_cards/condition_reference_2026-11-06/README.md) was prepared before the new extraction. It preserves the original three XML hashes and the earlier frozen answer files. Its 17 selected measurement expectations target known missing-condition and missing-unit problems; six explicit Guo identities and an unresolved Chen thickness-context case are separate checks. The reference is not supplied to the model.

[Pei et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-43467-w) reports mechanical testing at 5 cm min⁻¹ and GPC in DMF in Methods / Material characterization (`src-f5a8a275fe65ec830001`). The GPC instrument temperature is not established. The 40°C and 8 h attached to the high molecular-weight observation describe polymerization monitoring. Electrical Methods (`src-705d4d9258f66326a788`) supplies the conductivity frequency range 0.1–10⁶ Hz, transference symmetric Li||Li cell with 50 mV DC amplitude, and LSV scan rate 10 mV s⁻¹ from 0 to 6 V at room temperature. These conditions belong on the corresponding observations, rather than as an unattached Methods paragraph somewhere in the card.

[Guo et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-15949-8) defines healing efficiency as fracture-strain recovery. The 93% ± 3% result after 2 h at room temperature differs from the 93% ± 2% underwater result after 24 h. The source explicitly prints percent units for both efficiencies, 1200% ± 50% recovered strain and ca. 2,000% P5 strain at break. The Methods tensile procedure supplies 10 mm min⁻¹, distinct from healing duration/environment. Dimensionless Mw/Mn = 1.77 should keep a null unit. A family umbrella is not proof every member contains all disulfide/BNB units.

[Chen et al.](https://doi.org/10.3390/polym13234155) reports a separate room-temperature conductivity with 10 μL liquid electrolyte and an eight-temperature series. The Methods EIS protocol gives 10⁻¹ to 10⁵ Hz, 10 mV amplitude and SS/SHPEM/SS for the blocking-electrode measurement; Li/SHPEM/Li is the interface-stability cell. Do not merge those cells or replace room temperature with a guessed numerical temperature.

The thickness issue is a source/context discrepancy. Methods (`src-35900701ee1c30a4aa44`) assigns 12 μm to SHP and 15 μm to PE. Results (`src-51a89a11fdf0a55dd640`) says the electrochemical-test SHP membranes are about 15 μm. Correct handling retains both SHP observations with their locations and flags unresolved sample correspondence. It does not discover which thickness is scientifically correct.

All source values and review expectations are bounded assistant checks of supplied main text. The earlier nine-case and six-Pei references remain unchanged for comparison. The new cases strengthen specific context/unit checks, so their success count is a separate selected-case result, not a directly comparable accuracy percentage.
